How the luck numbers are calculated

A bracket simulation model using Pythagorean win% and the Log5 formula — same methodology used in MLB sabermetric analysis.

Step 1 — Team Strength
Pythagorean Win%
Measures true team quality from run differential, reducing noise from one-run game luck.
Pyth = RS1.83 / (RS1.83 + RA1.83)

RS = runs scored, RA = runs allowed. The 1.83 exponent is the baseball-calibrated value. A team scoring 800 and allowing 700 has Pyth ≈ .566.

Step 2 — Head-to-Head Probability
Log5 Formula
Bill James's formula for the probability that team A beats team B in a single game.
P(A beats B) = (A − A×B) / (A + B − 2×A×B)

Where A and B are each team's Pythagorean win%. Correctly reduces to A when B = .500 (league-average opponent).

Step 3 — Series Win Probability
Binomial Series Formula
Exact calculation — no simulation needed. Format auto-detected from actual series data.
P(win BO7) = Σk=4..7 C(k−1,3) × p4 × (1−p)k−4

p = single-game win probability from Log5. Sums over all ways to win exactly 4 games in 4–7 total. Series length (BO3/BO5/BO7) is detected automatically per round.

Step 4 — Full Bracket Path
Actual + Counterfactual Rounds
For rounds after elimination, the model follows the actual bracket to find real would-have-faced opponents.

Once a team is eliminated, the model traces who actually beat their eliminator through the remaining rounds. This gives the true counterfactual opponents — not random 50/50.

Championship probability = product of all round probabilities. For a Round 1 exit: R1 × counterfactual R2 × R3 × R4.

Step 5 — Career Statistics
Expected Championships
Exp = Σ P(champ)year

Sum of championship probabilities across all playoff appearances. Tracks across team changes — the GM, not the franchise.

Luck Delta & P(0 Rings)
Luck Δ = Actual − Expected
P(0 rings) = Π (1 − Pyear)

P(0 rings) multiplies per-year no-championship probabilities across the full career. Lower = more unlucky to still have zero titles.

Data Notes
Sources & Caveats
  • Game data: full league game history via StatsPlus API
  • GM histories: scraped from StatsPlus report pages for all active managers
  • Career tracking follows GMs across team changes
  • Minimum threshold: 3 playoff appearances required for inclusion
  • Years with incomplete game data are flagged ⚠ and championship probabilities are corrected using 50/50 estimates for missing rounds
